About GreenTrail
GREEN HEALTH SCORE

A composite index (0–100) derived from real OpenStreetMap data queried along your route. It combines 7 environmental and urban-quality dimensions:

0.25🌳 Naturetree density + park proximity + water bodies
0.15🛡 Safetylit streets + crossings + footways
0.15💨 Airdistance from motorways + industrial areas
0.13🚶 Walkabilitysidewalk coverage + pedestrian paths
0.12🪑 Comfortbenches/km + water + toilets + cafés
0.08🔇 Tranquilitylow major-road density
0.12+ moreactivity, biodiversity, scenic, night walk

The timeline at the bottom maps 7 grouped factor categories to actual OSM-detected features along your route.

HOW TO USE
1Allow location or use Berlin center as start.
2Map Point — tap map to set destination. By Distance — pick 5/10/15 km radius, app finds greenest spot inside.
3Return = same path back. Circular Tour = loop via green waypoint.
4Adjust Green Priority slider → more parks & nature vs. shortest path.
5Tap Find Green Route. Results appear in the Scores panel. Timeline blobs show where each factor occurs along the route — click a blob to jump there.
6Use Export → Google Maps or Apple Maps to navigate on foot.
